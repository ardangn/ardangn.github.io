Puggindoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Puggindoku ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android made by Red Koala ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

No account needed

You can play Puggindoku without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts, and we do not run our own servers that collect information about you.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: unlocked levels, stars and best times, power-ups, your Bone Stash and Bone Collector total, Daily Challenge results, and settings such as sound volume and language. It is deleted from the device when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.

Google Play Games Services (leaderboards and cloud save)

The game uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboards ("Biggest Bone Stash", "Daily Challenge" and "Bone Collector") and for cloud save. If you are signed in to Google Play Games:

  • your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and your scores are sent to Google and shown on the leaderboards to other players;
  • a copy of your game progress (the progress information listed above, except device settings such as sound and language) is saved in your Google account using Play Games Saved Games, so it can be restored after reinstalling the game or on a new device. Only you can access your saved game. You can delete it from your Google account (Google Play Games settings, or myactivity.google.com / your Google Account data settings).

If you are not signed in to Google Play Games, nothing is sent to Google Play Games and your progress stays on your device only. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games settings on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner during levels, full-screen ads between some levels and when the game starts, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward (a power-up, extra bones, or one extra bone in the Daily Challenge). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interactions with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Personalized ads and your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings (Privacy button).
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use analytics services. The game contains no in-app purchases and no chat.

Sharing and links

The Share button opens your phone's own share menu with a link to the game on Google Play; the game does not see who you share with. The game contains links to its Google Play page, to Red Koala's developer page on Google Play ("More games") and to this privacy policy. These open outside the game and are covered by those websites' own policies.

Children

Puggindoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for teens.

Security and retention

Information saved by the game stays on your device until you delete it. Information collected or stored by Google (Play Games, including your cloud save, and AdMob) is kept and protected according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
