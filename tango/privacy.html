About Tango

Tango is a sun-and-moon logic puzzle game with campaign puzzles, Daily Challenges and Endless mode. You can play without creating a Tango account. Google Play Games features are optional. This policy applies to Tango: Binary Logic Puzzle, Android package com.redkoala.tango, and its development/test builds.

Progress Stored On Your Device

The game stores puzzle progress, unfinished boards, completion times, settings and ad-frequency counters on your device using Android app storage. We do not operate a separate server that stores your puzzle saves. Android device backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up or restored.

Google Play Games And Leaderboards

If you use Google Play Games, Google authenticates your existing account and handles your Play Games profile. Tango uses your gamer name and player identifier to provide leaderboards, and sends game scores such as Endless streaks and adjusted challenge-completion times to Google. Other players may see your gamer name, rank and score according to Google's profile-visibility settings. The game also reads public leaderboard scores to display rankings. Tango does not request your Google password or a friends list, and does not implement Play Games cloud saves.

You can play the puzzles without using Google Play Games. You can manage profile visibility, game sign-in accounts and Play Games data through Google's settings.

Advertising And Consent

Tango uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads and ads between eligible sessions or levels. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device/account identifiers, app and ad interactions, crash information, diagnostics and performance data. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Tango does not request precise device location.

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before ads are requested. You can revisit available choices using Privacy choices in the game's settings. Available ad experiences and data use depend on your choices, region, device and Google's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Development builds currently use Google's test ad units.

How Data Is Used And Shared

Local progress supports gameplay and preferences. Play Games identity and scores support optional sign-in and rankings. Advertising identifiers, interactions and diagnostic data support the Google services described above. Google acts under its own terms and privacy policies; we do not sell your locally stored puzzle saves. Data transmitted by the integrated Google SDKs is encrypted in transit. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention And Deletion

Local data remains in app storage until you clear it, reset relevant progress or remove the app. Clearing app storage removes local progress and settings; device backups may be managed separately through Android/Google settings.

Google controls retention of information processed by its services. To delete Tango's Play Games data, use the Google Play Games profile/settings controls for deleting data for an individual game. Deleting Play Games data can permanently remove leaderboard records. Removing local app data does not by itself delete records held by Google, and deleting Play Games data does not by itself clear the game's local save.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to Red Koala, email redkoala64@gmail.com. We do not have access to delete your Google Account or Google's advertising records on your behalf.

Intended Audience

Tango is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Red Koala, contact us so we can review the request. Google's services may apply additional age and regional restrictions.

Changes And Contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Contact Red Koala at redkoala64@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.
