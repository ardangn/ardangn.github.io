Squish. Roll. Match.

Roll the jelly.
Match every face.

Every face of the jelly cube carries a symbol. Roll it tile by tile so the face that lands matches the tile below, grab keys, flip switches to raise bridges, jump through teleport pads and reach the exit in as few moves as you can.

Coming soon to Google Play
01

275 levels in 4 worlds

Start with a guided first level, then open new worlds as you go. Keys, switches, bridges, teleport pads and wildcard tiles join the mix, and every board can be solved by thinking it through. Match par to earn all three stars.

02

Endless and Hard Endless

Fresh boards that never run out. In Endless, clear board after board at par, first try and without a hint, to build your streak. Hard Endless shares one bank of spare moves across the whole run, so every wasted roll counts.

03

Zig Zag

Race up a path that crumbles away behind you. Pick the right turn with the right face down, keep ahead of the collapse and push your best distance further.

04

Leaderboards

Compare your best Zig Zag run, your best Hard Endless run and your longest Endless streak with other players through optional Google Play Games leaderboards.

Play at your own pace

Every level works offline. You get one free hint per level and can undo any move; optional rewarded ads give extra hints or show the full route. Jelly Roll contains ads and is intended for players aged 13 and older.

No Jelly Roll account is required; Google Play Games features are optional.
