About Jelly Roll

Jelly Roll is a 3D logic puzzle game: roll a jelly cube across puzzle boards so each face matches the tiles below. It has level worlds, Endless, Hard Endless and Zig Zag modes. You can play without creating a Jelly Roll account. Google Play Games features are optional. This policy applies to Jelly Roll, Android package com.redkoala.jellyroll, and its development/test builds.

Progress Stored On Your Device

The game stores level progress and stars, unlocked worlds, your current Endless, Hard Endless and Zig Zag runs and best results, and settings such as sound, language and controls on your device using Android app storage. We do not operate a server that stores your progress, and Jelly Roll does not use cloud saves. Android device backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up or restored.

Google Play Games And Leaderboards

If you use Google Play Games, Google authenticates your existing account and handles your Play Games profile. Jelly Roll uses your gamer name and player identifier to provide leaderboards, and sends game scores to Google: your best Zig Zag distance, your best Hard Endless run and your longest Endless streak. Other players may see your gamer name, rank and score according to Google's profile-visibility settings. The game may also read public leaderboard scores to display rankings. Jelly Roll does not request your Google password or a friends list.

You can play every puzzle without using Google Play Games. You can manage profile visibility, game sign-in accounts and Play Games data through Google's settings.

Advertising And Consent

Jelly Roll uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads (for hints, the full route and continuing a Hard Endless run) and ads between levels. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device/account identifiers, app and ad interactions, crash information, diagnostics and performance data. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Jelly Roll does not request precise device location.

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before ads are requested, and you can revisit available choices from the game's settings. Available ad experiences and data use depend on your choices, region, device and Google's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Development and early test builds may show Google's test ads or no ads.

How Data Is Used And Shared

Local progress supports gameplay and your preferences. Play Games identity and scores support optional sign-in and rankings. Advertising identifiers, interactions and diagnostic data support the Google services described above. Jelly Roll has no analytics of its own and sends nothing to Red Koala. Google acts under its own terms and privacy policies; we do not sell your data. Data transmitted by the integrated Google SDKs is encrypted in transit. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention And Deletion

Local data remains in app storage until you clear it or remove the app. Clearing app storage removes your progress and settings; device backups may be managed separately through Android/Google settings.

Google controls retention of information processed by its services. To delete Jelly Roll's Play Games data, use the Google Play Games profile/settings controls for deleting data for an individual game. Deleting Play Games data can permanently remove your leaderboard records. Removing local app data does not by itself delete records held by Google, and deleting Play Games data does not by itself clear the game's local progress.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to Red Koala, email redkoala64@gmail.com. We do not have access to delete your Google Account or Google's advertising records on your behalf.

Intended Audience

Jelly Roll is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Red Koala, contact us so we can review the request. Google's services may apply additional age and regional restrictions.

Changes And Contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Contact Red Koala at redkoala64@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.
